Privacy Policy for Wrench Rush: Junkyard Stripper

Last Updated: September 19, 2026

Wrench Rush: Junkyard Stripper ("we", "our", or "the game") is an arcade mobile game where players unbolt donor car parts before the overhead crusher descends. This Privacy Policy explains how our game handles user privacy.

1. Data Collection & User Data

Wrench Rush does not collect, track, or share any personal identifiable information (PII). We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, precise location, or browsing history.

2. Device Permissions

Wrench Rush requires no dangerous device permissions. It uses the device vibration motor (haptic feedback) to simulate bolt unzipping and rust breaking. No camera, microphone, or file system access is required.

3. Local Storage & Offline Game Saves

Your high scores, unlocked tools, and bank balance are stored locally on your device using browser local storage. You can reset or delete this game data at any time by clearing your app data or cache.

4. Third-Party Trademarks & Independence

Wrench Rush: Junkyard Stripper is an independent game. It is not affiliated with, authorized, or endorsed by LKQ Corporation, Pick Your Part, Milwaukee Tool, B'laster Corporation, or any automotive manufacturer. All product names, vehicle models, and trademarks are property of their respective owners and used solely for identification and simulation purposes.

5. Children's Privacy

Wrench Rush does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game contains no violence, blood, or vulgarity.
